PRIORITY 01 / THE PRODUCT TEST

Does better discovery pay?

Randomly assign households within direct and partner groups to the current or improved recommendation system. Keep prices, content, and promotions unchanged. Local reviewers check cultural relevance and misleading suggestions.

PRIORITY 02 / THE PARTNERSHIP TEST

Are the customers really new?

Expand Claro offers in stages after finance and privacy review. Randomly keep some eligible households on existing offers. Count partner payments, taxes, service costs, and losses from direct subscribers moving to lower-margin packages.

A · Existing offers
vs.
B · Expanded Claro offer
90-DAY PROPOSAL

Set sample sizes and success thresholds from baseline data before testing. Measure additional contribution per assigned household, retention, and complaints. Include development and rollout costs. Both tests must show credible financial benefits without worse customer outcomes.

About this research, its limits, and AI assistance

RESEARCH SCOPE & LIMITS

This site adapts the supplied Netflix AI Strategic Position Diagnostic and the shorter passages developed in the conversation. The industry is video streaming, with competition extending to social video and advertising budgets. Brazil and India are compared as growth opportunities, not first-entry markets.

The research snapshot is 25 October 2026. Country indicators are 2024 observations. The report does not provide Netflix’s internal country profitability, acquisition costs, actual Claro commercial terms, or measured returns from these proposals. The recommendation is conditional. The interactive cost model is an educational extension, not evidence collected for the report.
